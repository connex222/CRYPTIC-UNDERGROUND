## Included changes

- Corrected **16** demonstrated weak/invalid cryptic clue variants, including false palindrome claims and incorrect wordplay. The original answers and crossword crossing letters remain unchanged.
- Replaced **one exceptional future puzzle** with `cu-0282-r2` in the next recurrence of the slot on **21 November 2027**. Original `cu-0282` and its history from **9 October 2026** are preserved. The revised grid has **14 entries** and **20.5%** unchecked letters, versus **four entries** and **84.2%** before.
- Standard crossword entry numbering, improved keyboard/touch and across/down navigation, progressive hints, pencil marks, accessible controls and dialogues, corrected crossing-square error counts, guarded word reveals and shareable spoiler-free results.
- Finished crosswords cannot be altered, cleared or spoiled by subsequent keyboard, reveal or hint actions.
- Daily progress now uses absolute calendar-day keys with legacy first-cycle restoration; practice completion no longer changes the daily streak.
- Kept the complete standalone, offline-capable HTML game, all original daily/practice puzzles, existing visual identity and user data in local storage.

## Known follow-ups

1. **Human editorial review:** Expert assessment of the remaining clue surfaces, alternate defensible answers, rare abbreviations, UK homophones and the 59 flagged grids. A 40% unchecked rate is not by itself proof of an unfair grid.
2. **Physical accessibility acceptance:** NVDA on Windows, VoiceOver on iOS, TalkBack on Android, 200% zoom and touch ergonomics.
3. **Offline content disclosure:** Because future answers are embedded in the single offline file, users who inspect source can see them. True embargoes would require new content delivery architecture and a separate product decision.

## Maintenance and rollback

Use `docs/QA-FAIRNESS.md`, `docs/CORRECTION-LEDGER.md`, `docs/EDITORIAL-EXCEPTIONS.md` and `docs/GRID-EDITORIAL-REVIEW.md` as the canonical QA and editorial record. Before future changes, run the five Node suites and the Playwright browser job. To roll back an unexpected regression, create a reviewed revert of the squash commit or restore the known pre-release version; **do not delete user local storage or force-push `main`**.
